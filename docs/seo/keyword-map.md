## Recommended expansion: publish only with distinct original content

These are future recommendations, not routes created or submitted in the current sitemap. Avoid splitting a useful broad service page into thin near-duplicates. When a specific service page launches, narrow the broad page into a service overview and update its links and primary keyword focus.

| Proposed URL | Primary high-intent topic | Supporting commercial/local keywords | Content needed before publication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/services/custom-software` | Custom software development | custom software development services India; hire custom software developers; bespoke business software development; custom software development Nagpur | Requirements, architecture, web/business applications, delivery approach, real examples and enquiry CTA |
| `/services/business-automation` | Business process automation | business automation services Nagpur; business process automation company India; automate manual business processes; small business automation solutions | Specific workflows, integration boundaries, measurable evaluation approach and real implementation examples |
| `/services/workflow-digitization` | Digitising operational workflows | workflow digitization services; enterprise workflow automation software; approval workflow automation; replace spreadsheet workflows | Distinct approval/document/workflow scope, roles, audit trails and adoption process; merge with automation if overlap is too high |
| `/services/crm-erp-integration` | Connecting CRM/ERP systems | CRM ERP integration services; custom CRM development company Nagpur; ERP integration company India; sales workflow automation | Supported integrations that can actually be delivered, data mapping, migration, access controls and an example |
| `/locations/nagpur` | Verified local delivery context | business software development Nagpur; workflow automation company Nagpur; software developers Ramdaspeth | Actual location/contact information, genuine Nagpur delivery examples and local enquiry details; do not clone service copy |
| `/resources` | Practical business automation education | business automation guides; custom software buying guide | Original article index, readable navigation, relevant services and genuine dates |

International/offshore intent belongs in the custom-software page's remote delivery/process sections: **offshore custom software development India**, **remote software development team India**, **outsource business software development**, **B2B software development partner India**. Describe actual collaboration, availability and handover. Do not claim overseas offices, specific team capacity, accreditations or savings without evidence.

Keep existing ranking URLs. If consolidation requires moving a page, implement a permanent hosting-level redirect, update internal links/canonicals/sitemap, and monitor Search Console. Do not publish both equivalent URLs with competing copy.
